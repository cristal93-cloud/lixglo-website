LixGlo Media & Brand Assets Directory
=======================================

1. Official Logo:
   Place the uploaded logo file here with the exact name:
   `LOGO_LINEDESIGN.png`
   Path: public/assets/LOGO_LINEDESIGN.png

2. Future Photography & Media:
   Place real installation photos or videos here and configure them in:
   `src/config/mediaAssets.ts`

   Examples:
   - hero.jpg or hero.mp4
   - residential.jpg
   - commercial.jpg
   - installation.jpg
